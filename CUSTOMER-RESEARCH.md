# Customer research synthesis — Bodywise Companion

## Overall pattern supplied by the project owner

> Customers are not simply purchasing skincare products. They are purchasing solutions to specific personal concerns and expect those solutions to deliver noticeable results.

## Final problem statement

Customers lack clear and personalised guidance when choosing and using Be Bodywise products, resulting in mismatched expectations and dissatisfaction with product outcomes.

## Final How Might We

How might we help Be Bodywise customers choose the right products and use them correctly, so that their expectations align with realistic outcomes and they have a more satisfying customer experience?

## Additional retention observation supplied by the project owner

The owner reports observing that some consumers do not repurchase a product after the first purchase and instead move on to another product. A proposed response is to make the post-purchase experience more engaging through gamification and rewards.

This is recorded as an additional retention hypothesis. The underlying reasons for non-repurchase are not yet established here. Potential explanations to explore include: the product did not meet expectations; the original concern improved; the customer wants to try something new; the product was difficult or unpleasant to use; the product ran out and the customer forgot to replenish; price or availability; or the customer does not see a reason to remain loyal to one brand.

## Product implications

1. **Choose with clarity:** help customers understand product purpose, fit and limitations before buying.
2. **Use with confidence:** provide understandable directions, ingredient context and realistic expectations.
3. **Stay supported after purchase:** make routine guidance and progress reflection useful without promising results.
4. **Support appropriate replenishment:** offer a reminder based on the customer's own bottle estimate, with customer control. Never create urgency or automatic orders.
5. **Reward meaningful engagement, not consumption:** a prototype may award points for reading product guidance, sharing optional feedback and confirming responsible empty-pack disposal. Do not reward extra applications, faster depletion, buying more products or posting positive reviews.
6. **Keep brand rewards clearly labelled:** local demo points are not real discounts. Purchase-linked points, coupons, order verification and redemption require explicit Be Bodywise approval and a secure commerce integration.

## Validation still needed

- Ask customers why they did or did not repurchase, using neutral and non-leading questions.
- Separate customers who finished a product and were satisfied from those who switched because of dissatisfaction, price, availability or changing needs.
- Ask whether a reminder, routine support, refill option, loyalty benefit or nothing at all would genuinely help.
- Test whether rewards feel useful or manipulative, and whether they affect trust.
- Track appropriate measures such as repeat purchase within a defined period, product satisfaction, reminder opt-in/opt-out, and redemption only if a real approved programme exists.
- Do not infer causal impact from prototype points or self-reported in-app actions.
